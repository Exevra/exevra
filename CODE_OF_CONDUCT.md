# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, welcome differing experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and contributions rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and sharing private information without permission.

If you experience or witness a violation, report it through the project's private reporting channel once one is published. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where practical, and may remove content or participation to keep the community safe.

This project needs a maintainer to publish a private reporting contact before this policy can receive confidential reports.
